Why China's New Year address draws attention beyond borders

By Matteo Giovannini

CGTN - January 2, 2026
Peace doves are released at Tiananmen Square, Beijing, China, January 1, 2026. /CFP

Editor's note: Matteo Giovannini, a special commentator on current affairs for CGTN, is a finance professional at the Industrial and Commercial Bank of China, a non-resident associate fellow at the Center for China and Globalization, and a member of the Global Young Leaders Dialogue. The article reflects the author's views and not necessarily those of CGTN.

As the world stepped into 2026, Chinese President Xi Jinping's New Year address once again attracted close attention from the international community. While year-end speeches are a common political tradition worldwide, China's New Year message has increasingly become a moment of global reflection. The breadth of international media coverage and commentary this year underscored the broader reality that China's development path and policy direction are now deeply intertwined with global economic stability, governance debates and future growth prospects.

One of the main reasons for the strong international resonance lies in the address's emphasis on stability and strategic continuity. In a global environment marked by frequent political shifts, rising uncertainty, and policy volatility in many major economies, China's message projected a sense of long-term planning and institutional consistency. The address did not seek short-term applause but instead reinforced objectives that have been articulated and pursued over time. For international observers, this continuity is itself a source of reassurance, particularly at a moment when global confidence remains fragile.

Economic confidence formed another central pillar of the address and a key driver of its international impact. Xi highlighted China's steady progress in high-quality development, industrial upgrading and innovation, while acknowledging ongoing challenges in the global economy.

Foreign commentary widely noted the pragmatic tone of the speech, interpreting it as an effort to stabilize expectations rather than oversell performance. As many economies continue to grapple with sluggish growth, debt pressures and structural adjustment, China's focus on long-term fundamentals has drawn sustained attention from abroad.

The address's emphasis on technological innovation further contributed to its global resonance. In recent years, innovation has become a shared priority across both developed and developing economies, particularly in areas such as digital transformation, green technologies and advanced manufacturing. While Western narratives often frame China's innovation drive through the lens of competition, the New Year address presented it as an essential pathway toward productivity growth and improved living standards. This framing aligned with global debates on how innovation can serve broader economic and social goals, helping the message connect with audiences well beyond China.

Another important reason for the speech's strong response was its people-centered focus. Xi reiterated the importance of employment, social security and improving quality of life, themes that carry broad relevance across countries.

Overseas observers noted that this emphasis reflects a governance approach that places social stability and long-term welfare at the core of modernization. In a world where inequality, aging populations, and social fragmentation pose growing challenges, China's articulation of development as a process aimed at benefiting the broader population struck a chord internationally.

The address also resonated because of its global perspective. Xi reaffirmed China's commitment to openness, multilateralism and international cooperation at a time when global governance mechanisms face increasing strain. External observers acknowledged that despite geopolitical differences, China continues to position itself as a supporter of dialogue and cooperation on issues ranging from economic development to climate change. For many countries, particularly in the Global South, this message reinforces China's role as a contributor to global growth and shared development.

China's expanding global influence further explains why the New Year address was so closely followed. As a major trading partner, investment destination and source of innovation, China's policy signals matter to governments, businesses and markets worldwide. Foreign media attention reflects the recognition that China's domestic priorities are increasingly shaping global outcomes. In this context, the New Year address is not viewed simply as a ceremonial speech, but as an important reference point for understanding China's trajectory in the year ahead.

Notably, the address avoided confrontational rhetoric. Rather than emphasizing ideological divisions or geopolitical rivalry, it focused on common challenges and shared responsibilities. This tone contrasted with the increasingly polarized political discourse elsewhere and enhanced the speech's international appeal. By emphasizing cooperation over confrontation, the address offered a narrative of stability and mutual benefit that resonated with audiences seeking constructive engagement in an uncertain world.

Finally, the strong international response reflects a growing appreciation for clarity and coherence in global leadership. The address presented a comprehensive vision that linked economic transformation, social development and international cooperation into a unified framework. Overseas media reactions, whether analytical or cautiously positive, demonstrated an understanding that China's approach, regardless of differing perspectives, is grounded in long-term planning and consistent execution.

In a global landscape marked by fragmentation and uncertainty, Xi's New Year address stood out for its calm confidence and forward-looking orientation. Its resonance beyond China's borders stems not from dramatic policy shifts, but from its clear articulation of continuity, stability and openness. That combination explains why the New Year message continues to generate strong international attention and why it remains an important moment for understanding China's role in shaping the global future.
